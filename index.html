F2Break a chapter or a scene at the cursor.
Highlight, F2Make a character, a place, a note. Part of a word will do.
The gold dotWord range, change the break, remove it.
Prompt / ExpandAda, on the chapter or scene they sit beside. Not connected yet — shown quiet until she is.
Also calledMr. Toad and Toad, one character.
IndentFirst lines set in, as a book does.
DetailsThe dashed lines under what you have recorded.
Break hereThe same as F2, for the mouse.
Word budgetEach chapter’s share of the book’s range, taken from Basics. Red means over, never stopped.
